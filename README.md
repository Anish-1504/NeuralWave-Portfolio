# KINETIC LABS — Ultra-Premium 3D Interactive Technology Showcase

An Awwwards-level interactive digital product showcase website featuring the **Sony WH-1000XM6** wireless noise-cancelling headphones and the **Apple Watch Ultra** smartwatch.

## ✨ Highlights & Key Features

1. **Seamless Master Toggle Switch**:
   - Easily flip between **Sony WH-1000XM6** and **Apple Watch Ultra** at any time from both the navigation bar and the hero section.
   - Dynamic fluid ambient glow and colorway transitions (Deep Sony Cyan/Blue vs. Apple Sky Blue/Titanium).

2. **Scroll-Driven 118-Frame 1080p Cinematic Sequences**:
   - Preloaded high-resolution frame sequences for both products with smooth requestAnimationFrame canvas rendering.
   - Interactive scrub controls, auto-rotation toggle, frame counter, and hot-spot annotations at critical engineering telemetry points.

3. **Sony WH-1000XM6 Interactive Suite**:
   - **Active Noise Cancellation Simulator**: Live Web Audio API synthesizer for Airplane Cabin, Open Office, and Subway traffic noise with real-time anti-phase wave oscilloscope.
   - **Sound Technology & Acoustics**: Carbon fiber composite 30mm dome driver breakdown, LDAC 990 kbps vs SBC 328 kbps interactive bandwidth visualizer, and DSEE Ultimate AI upscaling.
   - **Engineering Specifications**: Blueprint drawer covering drivers, NC processors, battery life, and codecs.

4. **Apple Watch Ultra Interactive Suite**:
   - **Watch Face Studio**: Live interactive watch faces (Modular Ultra with live clock, Wayfinder Compass, Activity Analog, and Tactical Night Vision).
   - **Finishes & Bands Customizer**: Natural Titanium, Dark Graphite, Ceramic Pearl finishes, and Ocean/Trail/Alpine loops.
   - **Interactive Activity Rings Simulator**: Move, Exercise, and Stand rings with interactive sliders that animate and celebrate goal completion.
   - **Biosensing Cluster & ECG Telemetry**: Live sinus rhythm ECG waveform canvas with heart rate telemetry, SpO2 blood oxygen, and wrist temperature sensors.
   - **Titanium Specifications**: Blueprint drawer covering aerospace titanium, 3,000-nit LTPO OLED, and S9 SiP neural engine.

5. **Cross-Flagship Comparison Matrix (`/compare`)**:
   - Side-by-side engineering breakdown across 8 distinct architectural categories.
